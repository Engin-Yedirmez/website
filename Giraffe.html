Giraffe

Scientific Name: Giraffa camelopardalis

Habitat: African savannas and open woodlands

Diet: Leaves (mostly from acacia trees), fruits, flowers

Fun Fact: Giraffes use their long necks to reach high trees. They have a 45cm long tongue!
