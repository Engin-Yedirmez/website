Crocodile

Scientific Name: Crocodylus niloticus

Diet: Carnivore, hunts fish, birds, and mammals

Habitat: Rivers, lakes, and wetlands across Africa and Asia

Fun Fact: Crocodiles can live for decades and can hold their breath underwater for over an hour!
