Elephant

Scientific Name: Loxodonta africana

Habitat: Sub-Saharan Africa – forests, grasslands, and savannas

Diet: Herbivorous – grasses, leaves, bark, and fruit

Fun Fact: Elephants are the largest land animals and have incredible memory and social bonds.
