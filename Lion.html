Lion

Scientific Name: Panthera leo

Habitat: Grasslands, savannas, and open woodlands in Africa

Diet: Carnivorous – zebras, buffalo, antelopes

Fun Fact: Lions live in prides and are known as the "King of the Jungle", though they mostly live in grasslands!
